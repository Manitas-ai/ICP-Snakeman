THE STORY

Where It All Began

† IN MEMORIAM

SNAKEMAN SR.

The Original. The Legend. The Father.

Marcus "Mako" Vega gained his serpent powers after a classified government experiment gone wrong in 1998. For 20 years he protected the city as Snakeman — crawling skyscrapers, neutralising criminals with precision venom strikes, and never losing a single battle.

Until a Tuesday evening in March 2022. Returning home in civilian clothes, no suit, no powers activated — a rogue truck lost its brakes on Route 9. Marcus never saw it coming. The greatest hero of his generation was gone in seconds.

"He didn't die in battle. He died being human. That's what made him the greatest of all."
⚡ ACTIVE HERO

SNAKEMAN JUNIOR

The Heir. The Evolution. The Unstoppable.

Leo Vega was only 16 when his father died. Grief-stricken and furious, he broke into his father's secret laboratory — and touched the same serpentine serum that had given Marcus his powers. But Leo's younger DNA absorbed it differently. More completely.

Within 72 hours, Leo could partially transform into a 40-foot anaconda. His venom was ten times more potent. His skin regenerated damage in real-time. He could communicate with every snake on Earth simultaneously. His father's legacy didn't just live on — it evolved.

"They said I had big shoes to fill. Dad didn't wear shoes. He shed his skin and became something greater. So will I."

SERPENT SPEED

Sees in thermal infrared like a pit viper, detects heartbeats through walls up to 200 metres away, and senses chemical traces left up to 72 hours earlier. No one hides from him.
